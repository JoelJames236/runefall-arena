How to Play

WASD Move & strafe
Mouse Turn camera & champion
Left Click Basic attack where the dot in the middle points (one swing per click · hold to keep swinging)
1–6 Abilities (rebind them in Settings, e.g. to R, F or the side mouse buttons)
Tab Cycle enemy targets
F1 / F2 Friendly target: self / ally
Space Jump · Shift Sprint
Wheel Zoom · Esc Menu
  • Basic attacks are aimed. Every left click swings (or shoots) where the dot in the middle of the screen points — it hits whichever enemy hitbox is under the dot, or nothing. The dot turns red while an enemy your attack can reach is under it. Melee reaches 4.5 yd in front of you; ranged shots fly straight and break on pillars. Every champion has the same hitbox, however big the model. Hold the button to keep swinging; the swing timer sets the pace. The enemy you click on also becomes your target for abilities.
  • Hits from behind deal 10% more damage. Critical hits (8% chance) deal 50% more.
  • Casting requires standing still — moving or jumping cancels it. Break line of sight behind the rune pillars.
  • Stuns, disorients and Shield Slam interrupt casts. Crowd control has diminishing returns (100% → 50% → 25% → immune).
  • In 2v2, heals and shields go to your friendly target: F2 selects your ally, F1 yourself.
  • After 60 seconds, Dampening steadily reduces healing and shields so every round ends.
