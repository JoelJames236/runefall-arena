How to Play

WASD Move & strafe
Mouse Turn camera & champion
Left Click Basic attack where the dot in the middle points (one swing per click · hold to keep swinging)
1–6 Abilities (rebind them in Settings, e.g. to R, F or the side mouse buttons)
Tab Cycle enemy targets
F1 / F2 Friendly target: self / ally
Space Jump · Shift Sprint
Wheel Zoom · Esc Menu
G Battleground: drop the flag · hold B scoreboard
  • Basic attacks are aimed. Every left click swings (or shoots) where the dot in the middle of the screen points — it hits whichever enemy hitbox is under the dot, or nothing. The dot turns red while an enemy your attack can reach is under it. Melee reaches 4.5 yd in front of you; ranged shots fly straight and break on pillars. Ranged aim assist: with the dot on or close to an enemy, your shot leads a moving target by itself and bends a little towards it in flight — the dot turns red when it will. Every champion has the same hitbox, however big the model — and pets / summons such as the necromancer's ghoul can be hit on their own. Hold the button to keep swinging; the swing timer sets the pace. The enemy you click on also becomes your target for abilities.
  • Hits from behind deal 10% more damage. Critical hits (8% chance) deal 50% more.
  • Casting requires standing still — moving or jumping cancels it. Break line of sight behind the rune pillars.
  • Stuns, disorients and Shield Slam interrupt casts. Crowd control has diminishing returns (100% → 50% → 25% → immune).
  • In 2v2, heals and shields go to your friendly target: F2 selects your ally, F1 yourself.
  • 5v5 Battleground — Thornwater Gorge. Run into the enemy keep, touch their flag in the upper flag room and carry it home to your own flag stand — it only counts while your own flag is at home. Three captures win; after 15 minutes the leader wins. The dead return in waves every 20 s at their graveyard. A dropped flag is returned by touching it (or goes home after 10 s). If both flags are out for over a minute, both carriers become Exposed and take more damage. Glowing power-ups appear every minute in mirrored pairs.
  • After 60 seconds, Dampening steadily reduces healing and shields so every round ends.
