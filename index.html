  • Casting a spell with a cast time requires standing still — moving or jumping cancels it.
  • Break line of sight behind the four rune pillars to stop enemy casts.
  • Interrupt key spells: stuns, disorients and Shield Slam cancel casts.
  • Crowd control has diminishing returns: repeated stuns last 50%, then 25%, then you are immune for 15s.
  • After 60 seconds, Dampening steadily reduces all healing and shields so every round ends.
  • Melee abilities need you to face your opponent.
